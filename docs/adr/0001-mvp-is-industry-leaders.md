# The product is an industry leaders finder

`docs/PLANNING.md` describes a later brand-diagnosis product: a business submits its own brand, sees why Google did not recommend it, and pays to unlock the full report. The MVP in `docs/MVP.md` replaces that for this codebase. A visitor enters an industry keyword and gets a free ranked list of leaders. Payments, accounts, PDFs, and brand diagnosis stay out until that later product is specified on its own.
