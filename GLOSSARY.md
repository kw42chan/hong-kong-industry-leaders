# Industry leaders

A visitor names an industry. The product shows which businesses Google Hong Kong is surfacing as the leaders of that industry.

## Language

**Industry keyword**:
The words a visitor types for one industry or service, in Chinese or English.
_Avoid_: Brand, category, query

**Search**:
One run for one industry keyword. It is queued, running, ready, or failed.
_Avoid_: Lead, report, job

**Search token**:
The unguessable key in the results link. Knowing it is enough to read that search. There is no account.
_Avoid_: Session, user id

**Recommendation query**:
One of the Hong Kong-style searches generated from the industry keyword, such as a "best X in Hong Kong" phrasing.
_Avoid_: Prompt, SERP task

**AI Overview**:
The AI summary box Google may show above organic results. It can name businesses and cite pages.
_Avoid_: AIO as a product name in visitor-facing copy, ChatGPT answer, Gemini answer

**Leader**:
A business domain that earns a place on the ranked list because it showed up in AI Overviews or the organic top results. Directories, news, and other third parties are not leaders.
_Avoid_: Brand, competitor, result

**Cited source**:
A third-party domain that keeps appearing in AI Overview citations or organic results, listed separately from leaders.
_Avoid_: Aggregator in visitor-facing copy, reference

**Snapshot**:
The set of Google Hong Kong results captured for one search at one time. It is not a live or personalized view.
_Avoid_: Report, audit
